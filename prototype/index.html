Prime Music prototype

An interactive recreation of the Prime Music screens and flows from my case study.

Design prototype by Suvarna Myneni, for portfolio purposes, not an Amazon product. All artwork, songs, artists, and playlists are placeholder example content. There is no sign-in, no payment, and no audio.

Screens

    Flows to try

    1. Welcome → Prime Music: tap Continue on the welcome screen.
    2. Moods & genres: Playlists tab → tap “All Moods & Genres” → pick a mood, such as “Hard & Heavy”.
    3. Add to library: open an album → “Add album to library” → it processes, a toast appears, and buttons change to In Library.
    4. Re-find it: menu (☰) → Your Library → Albums → the album you added → “more Prime songs available from this album”.
    5. Play: tap any song or ▶ → Now Playing with play/pause, next/previous, and a scrubber; the mini player persists.
    6. Wayfinding: ☰ shows Prime as its own section above Your Library and Music Store.
    7. Upsell: switch to Non-Prime, open the Music Store, and tap a blue Prime button.
